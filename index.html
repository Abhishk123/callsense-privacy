CallSense AI – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026
Package Name: com.callsense.ai.callsense_ai

CallSense AI is built as a privacy-first on-device call screening and fraud awareness application. Your contacts, incoming caller numbers, call logs, and custom screening rules stay strictly on your Android device and are never uploaded to external CallSense servers.

1. Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Call Screening Role (ROLE_CALL_SCREENING): Evaluates incoming phone numbers locally in real time to apply your allow, silence, reject, or fraud awareness preferences.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Checks locally whether an incoming caller is saved in your phonebook so trusted contacts always ring normally, and enables the optional contact picker when restricting a number. Contact lists are never uploaded or shared.
  • Call Log (READ_CALL_LOG): Displays your recent call history inside the Activity tab so you can inspect calls and block or silence spammers with one tap. Call logs remain strictly local on your device.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Shows silent top-screen Fraud & Risk Awareness banners, Unknown Caller alerts, Blocked Call notifications, and optional 1-tap Quick SMS launcher actions (opening your default SMS app cleanly without background SMS permissions).

2. Data Collection & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

CallSense AI itself operates no backend servers and does not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data, contacts, phone numbers, or call history.

Free-tier banner and opt-in rewarded ads are provided using the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location (derived from IP address), app interaction events, and crash/performance diagnostics strictly for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention & Control

All app settings, custom rules, and restricted contacts are stored locally via Android SharedPreferences. You can reset or erase all saved rules and restricted contacts anytime from the Shield tab (Reset Settings) or by uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

CallSense AI is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how CallSense AI works, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store listing.
